# Product Strategy One-Pager & OKRs — Module 1

## Scenario

**Fable Growth — B2C Mental Wellness**

Fable is a mental wellness app for adults aged 25–45. Its core experience successfully helps users through periods of acute stress and anxiety, but many highly engaged users stop using the product once that immediate need is resolved. The strategic challenge is to define what Fable becomes after the acute phase and create a durable engagement model without losing the simplicity and trust that made the product successful.

---


## 1. Playing to Win cascade

| Question | Your choice |
|---|---|
| **Winning aspiration** | Help adults who have moved beyond acute anxiety continue to feel in control of their everyday mental wellbeing through a simple, trusted Fable experience they choose to return to. |
| **Where to play** | Focus on existing Fable users aged 25–45 transitioning beyond their initial 90–120-day acute-use phase, within Fable’s existing mobile experience and freemium/premium model. Prioritise ongoing meditation, sleep, breathing and proactive wellbeing use cases rather than clinical treatment, B2B expansion or new-market expansion. |
| **How to win** | Combine Fable’s trusted, judgement-free experience with its understanding of each user’s engagement history to deliver personalised, low-friction post-acute check-ins, recommendations and wellbeing routines that remain useful after the original anxiety need has subsided. |
| **Capabilities required** | Build strong post-acute lifecycle detection, behavioural segmentation, personalised recommendations, AI-assisted check-ins, contextual re-engagement and experimentation capabilities while retaining Fable’s meditation and sleep content foundation and clinical-advisory safety guardrails. |
| **Management systems** | Track post-acute retention, weekly active usage and premium renewal by cohort; review experiment and engagement performance monthly; monitor personalisation and safety signals; and conduct a quarterly strategy review to decide whether the post-acute proposition is creating durable user value. |

## 2. Your one hard no

**We will not build a social/community layer as the primary growth strategy this quarter.**

Although community could create additional engagement, introducing a social model would materially change Fable’s private and simple user experience before we have proven that personalised post-acute wellbeing can create durable retention for the users we already serve.

---

## 3. OKR cascade

_One Objective and three Key Results that flow directly from the cascade. Each KR must be a measurable **outcome**, not an output/milestone._

> **Objective:** Make Fable a trusted ongoing wellbeing companion that users continue to value after their acute mental-wellness need has passed.
>
> - **KR1:** 180-day retention among users entering the post-acute phase from the current pre-initiative cohort baseline to a **30% relative improvement** by the end of Q4.
> - **KR2:** Weekly active usage among post-acute users from the current pre-initiative cohort baseline to a **25% relative improvement** by the end of Q4.
> - **KR3:** Premium renewal among users entering the post-acute phase from the current pre-initiative cohort baseline to a **20% relative improvement** by the end of Q4.
