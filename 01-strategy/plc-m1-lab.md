# Craft an Advanced Product Strategy, Module 1 Lab

## Write your one-page strategy
- **Winning aspiration: define winning in the customer's terms, not internal metrics. What does success look like for the specific person you serve?:** Fable becomes the trusted mental-wellness companion adults continue to use after acute anxiety subsides, helping them maintain everyday wellbeing through simple, personalised routines rather than returning only when they are struggling.
- **Where to play: segment, geography, and use case, explicitly. Which customers, markets, and surfaces will you focus on?:** Existing Fable users aged 25–45 transitioning beyond their initial 90–120-day acute-use phase, within Fable’s existing mobile, freemium and premium experience and current markets.
- **How to win: your differentiator. What can you do that your specific competitors cannot easily replicate?:** Use Fable’s trusted, judgement-free experience and first-party understanding of each user’s engagement journey to deliver personalised, low-friction post-acute check-ins and recommendations around meditation, sleep and breathing—turning Fable from an acute-support tool into an ongoing wellbeing habit without becoming clinical.
- **Capabilities required: what you must be world-class at. What will you build, buy, or partner for?:** Build post-acute lifecycle detection, personalised recommendation and AI-assisted check-in capabilities; strengthen behavioural segmentation, experimentation and notification timing; retain Fable’s core meditation/sleep content; and use clinical advisers to establish safety and non-clinical guardrails.
- **Management systems: the metrics and rituals that keep your choices alive quarter to quarter.:** Track post-acute cohort retention, weekly active usage and premium renewal; review cohort performance monthly; monitor personalisation quality and safety signals; and run a quarterly strategy review to determine whether the post-acute proposition is creating durable engagement.

## Name your one hard no
- **Your one hard no:** We will not build a social/community layer as the core growth strategy because it would shift Fable away from the private, simple and trusted experience that made it successful before we have proven that personalised post-acute wellbeing can create a durable individual habit.

## Write your three OKRs
- **Objective:** Make Fable the trusted ongoing wellbeing companion users continue to value after their acute need has passed.
- **KR1:** Increase 180-day retention among users who reach the post-acute stage by 30% versus the pre-initiative cohort by the end of Q4.
- **KR2:** Increase weekly active usage among post-acute users by 25% versus the pre-initiative baseline by the end of Q4.
- **KR3:** Increase premium renewal among users entering the post-acute phase by 20% versus the pre-initiative baseline by the end of Q4.
